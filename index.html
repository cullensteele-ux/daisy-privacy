Daisy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Daisy is a personal AI assistant operated by its sole developer, Cullen Steele, for his own use.

Data accessed

Daisy uses Google OAuth to access the developer's own Gmail and Google Calendar data solely to provide personal assistant functions — reading and triaging email, drafting messages, and managing calendar events on the developer's behalf.

Data use and storage

All data is processed and stored on the developer's own privately operated server. OAuth tokens and accessed data are never shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party. No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind is performed.

Users

This application has a single user — its developer. It is not offered to the public.
