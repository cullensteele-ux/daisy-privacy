Aspen QuickBooks Connector

Private integration for Aspen Leadership Advisors

This connector links a single QuickBooks Online company to its developer's own privately operated server. It is not offered to the public.

Connect, reconnect and disconnect

Connection and reconnection are performed by the developer through Intuit's OAuth authorization. To disconnect, revoke the app's access from within QuickBooks Online; stored tokens are then deleted from the developer's server.
