Aspen QuickBooks Connector - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

The Aspen QuickBooks Connector is a private integration operated by its sole developer, Cullen Steele, for the internal bookkeeping of Aspen Leadership Advisors.

Data accessed

The connector uses Intuit OAuth to access the developer's own QuickBooks Online company data (such as accounts, transactions, invoices, customers, vendors and financial reports) solely to support the developer's own bookkeeping and reporting.

Data use and storage

Data and OAuth tokens are processed and stored on the developer's own privately operated server. They are never sold, shared with or disclosed to any third party. No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind is performed.

Users

This application has a single user, its developer. It is not offered to the public.
